01 / About

About ONCRN关于ONCRN

中文简介 ↓

The Optic Neuritis Collaborative Research Network (ONCRN) is an investigator-led, ophthalmology-centered, multicenter collaborative research network dedicated to advancing clinical and translational research in optic neuritis.

ONCRN grew out of the multicenter collaboration established through GX-ICON (NCT07623252), a prospective multicenter study evaluating the 2022 International Criteria for Optic Neuritis and an antibody-stratified adjunct diagnostic approach. The network developed from the collaborative research framework established among ophthalmology teams at participating centers.

ONCRN is currently centered on ophthalmologists and neuro-ophthalmologists, working in collaboration with colleagues in neurology, medical imaging, and related disciplines.

ONCRN focuses on diagnostic criteria, clinical phenotyping, etiological classification, antibody testing, OCT and MRI assessment, acute treatment, and treatment accessibility in optic neuritis.

ONCRN emphasizes rigorous methodology, standardized data collection, transparent collaboration, responsible data use, and clinically meaningful research. Its long-term aim is to address important questions in optic neuritis that are difficult for individual centers to answer reliably on their own.

关于ONCRN

视神经炎研究协作网络（Optic Neuritis Collaborative Research Network，ONCRN）是由研究者发起、以眼科和神经眼科为核心的多中心研究协作网络，主要围绕视神经炎开展临床及相关转化研究。

ONCRN源于GX-ICON（NCT07623252）多中心前瞻性研究所形成的协作基础。GX-ICON主要评价2022年国际视神经炎诊断标准及抗体分层辅助诊断路径，ONCRN在参与中心眼科研究团队既有协作基础上逐步发展建立。

ONCRN目前以眼科医生和神经眼科医生为核心，与神经内科、医学影像等相关学科开展协作。

ONCRN重点关注视神经炎的诊断标准、临床表型、病因分层、抗体检测、OCT与MRI评估、急性期治疗及治疗可及性等问题。

网络重视严谨的研究方法、规范的数据采集、透明的协作机制、负责任的数据使用以及具有临床意义的研究结果，重点解决单一中心难以可靠回答的重要视神经炎临床问题。

03 / GX-ICON

GX-ICON多中心研究协作基础

Prospective Evaluation of the 2022 International Criteria for Optic Neuritis and an Antibody-Stratified Adjunct Diagnostic Approach

2022年国际视神经炎诊断标准及抗体分层辅助诊断路径的前瞻性评价

A prospective multicenter observational diagnostic accuracy study. No experimental treatment is assigned by the study.

一项前瞻性多中心观察性诊断准确性研究，研究不分配试验性治疗。

ClinicalTrials.gov Identifier: NCT07623252 ↗

Registered study title / 登记研究标题

A Multicenter Prospective Diagnostic Accuracy Study of the 2022 International Criteria for Optic Neuritis and an Antibody-Stratified Adjunct in Acute or Subacute Visual Loss When Optic Neuritis Is Suspected in China

GX-ICON registry: ClinicalTrials.gov. Record checked on 23 September 2026; last update posted 15 June 2026.
